Life's Good Without Spying

A webOS homebrew app that switches off the surveillance stack on rooted LG TVs: ad delivery, Automatic Content Recognition, and always-on voice capture.

Features

  • Blocks LG ad, ACR, and telemetry domains by bind-mounting a generated /etc/hosts that maps them to loopback, with per-category toggles (including crash/diagnostic reporting, QuickSet, and optional lists for ThinQ / companion services, Home app sessions, and the LG Content Store).
  • Keeps UEI QuickSet remote-code lookup available by default; an optional QuickSet toggle can block it.
  • Delays blocking LG Service Delivery Platform (SDP) clock hosts for 60 seconds, while applying all other domain blocks immediately, so that webOS can set its clock first.
  • Optionally routes ConnMan's IPv4 DNS through a reversible local filter that uses the same domain blocklist. When the filter is enabled, a separate switch can also disable IPv6 in ConnMan for stricter DNS coverage.
  • Stops the on-TV ad, ACR, and telemetry daemons (admanager, livepick, acr2, nudge, rdxd, uploadd, sportsalarm).
  • Stops the LG voice and NLP services (voiceinput, voiceconductor, nlpmanager, performer) and the voice app.
  • Bind-mounts an inert stub over each target binary so that the Luna hub cannot respawn it, without needing a polling loop.
  • Answers calls to admanager, voiceconductor, and voiceinput with a small stand-in so that apps and Settings don't hang waiting on them.
  • With the ThinQ / companion toggle on, also stops the assistant and IoT daemons (trigger_thinq, trigger_alexa, iot-client, iot-proxy).
  • Disabling protection restores the stopped services and the UPnP/SSDP daemons, rather than leaving them down until a reboot.
  • Optionally swaps /usr/sbin/upnpd for an inert stub, killing UPnP discovery both ways (which also breaks casting to the TV).
  • Wipes locally buffered ACR / speech-to-text files.
  • Re-applies everything at boot through a Homebrew Channel init.d hook.

Requirements

  • A rooted LG webOS (4+) TV with the Homebrew Channel installed.
  • Tested on: OLED65G45LW (EU), webOS TV 9.2.4, firmware 23.23.30.
  • Tested on webOS 4.4+

Uninstall

Select "Disable protection" in the app before you uninstall it. Uninstalling alone leaves protection on until the next reboot. Settings stay in /var/lib/webosbrew/ either way; the README explains how to remove them.
