Life's Good Without Spying

Switches off the surveillance stack on rooted LG TVs: ad delivery, Automatic Content Recognition, and always-on voice capture.

What it does

  • Sinks LG ad, ACR and telemetry domains to loopback, per category.
  • Stops the on-TV ad services (admanager, adoverlay, livepick).
  • Stops the LG voice services (voiceinput, voiceconductor).
  • Wipes locally buffered ACR and speech-to-text residue.
  • Re-applies everything at boot.

Everything is a runtime change. Nothing writes to the signed system partitions. Disabling the app, or removing it and rebooting, restores the stock setup. Requires a rooted TV with the Homebrew Channel.

Use

The headline shows the state. Each protection flips individually, the main button cycles Enable, Update and Disable, and Self-test proves it on the device: blocked names resolve to loopback, outbound connects fail, voice and ad services are down.
